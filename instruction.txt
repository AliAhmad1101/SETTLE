# 3. THE PROBLEM

Small businesses, student startups, D2C businesses, freelancers, agencies, and early-stage companies often receive payments digitally.

However, payment collection and business records can become disconnected.

For example:

A business may have:

Order #1042
₹1,999
Customer: Rahul
Expected payment: ₹1,999

Payment records may contain:

Payment ID: pay_xxxx
Amount: ₹1,999
Status: Captured

Another order might contain:

Order #1043
₹2,499
Expected payment: ₹2,499

But the payment system could contain:

₹2,499
Reference associated with another order

Or:

* Payment captured but order marked unpaid
* Order cancelled but payment captured
* Duplicate payment
* Partial payment
* Payment amount mismatch
* Refund issued but internal record still shows paid
* Payment exists without a matching order
* Order exists without a matching payment
* Failed payment incorrectly treated as successful

Finding these manually is tedious.

The real problem is not simply:

“Where is my payment?”

The real problem is:

**“What happened to my money/order, why doesn't my record match, and what should I do about it?”**

---

# 4. THE SOLUTION

SETTLE is an AI-powered reconciliation agent.

The user uploads or imports two datasets:

### Dataset A — Payments

Example:

* payment_id
* order_id
* amount
* status
* customer
* timestamp
* refund_amount

### Dataset B — Orders

Example:

* order_id
* customer
* expected_amount
* order_status
* order_date

SETTLE automatically:

1. Reads the datasets
2. Normalizes the records
3. Matches payments with orders
4. Detects discrepancies
5. Classifies discrepancies
6. Uses AI to explain suspicious cases
7. Assigns a severity
8. Recommends the next action
9. Generates a reconciliation summary
10. Lets the user inspect individual cases

The AI must behave like an **operations agent**, not merely an assistant.

---

# 5. IMPORTANT AI REQUIREMENT

AI must perform meaningful work.

DO NOT simply create:

“Ask our AI anything about your payments.”

Instead, AI should actively analyze structured business data.

The deterministic system should first calculate factual discrepancies.

Then the AI should:

* interpret the discrepancy
* classify the likely issue
* explain it in human language
* recommend an action
* generate a concise resolution note

For example:

Input:

Order:
ORD-1042
Expected: ₹2,499
Status: Paid

Payment:
pay_9182
Amount: ₹2,499
Status: Captured

But payment is associated with ORD-1041.

AI output:

**Possible mislinked payment**

“This payment amount exactly matches ORD-1042, but the payment reference is associated with ORD-1041. Verify the payment reference before updating the order.”

Recommended action:

**Review payment mapping**

Severity:

**Medium**

This demonstrates genuine AI reasoning over application data.
